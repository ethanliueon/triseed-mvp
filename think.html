The 5-Step Process

Step 1: Identifying an Unmet Need

Finding real, everyday health and wellness struggles that have not been adequately solved by current market offerings.

Step 2: Evidence Review

Reading, aggregating, and evaluating existing medical literature and peer-reviewed scientific data to establish what works.

Step 3: Concept Brainstorm

Designing and prototyping practical, user-friendly product concepts that prioritize the end-user experience and is scientifically tested.

Step 4: Feasibility Check

Ensuring that the product idea is entirely safe, affordable to manufacture at scale, and compliant with current regulatory boundaries.

Step 5: Responsible Translation

Researching safe manufacturing paths and evaluation frameworks to bring the final idea to life cleanly and responsibly.
