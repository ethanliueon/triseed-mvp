How we think

Every idea we work on goes through the same five steps.

Step 1: Identifying an unmet need

Finding real, everyday health and wellness struggles that have not been adequately solved by current products.

Step 2: Evidence review

Reading, aggregating, and evaluating existing medical literature and peer-reviewed research to understand what works.

Step 3: Concept generation

Designing and prototyping practical, user-friendly product concepts that put the end user first.

Step 4: Feasibility check

Checking that a concept can be made safely and affordably at scale, and that it fits within current regulatory boundaries.

Step 5: Responsible translation

Researching safe manufacturing paths and evaluation frameworks to bring the final idea to life responsibly.
