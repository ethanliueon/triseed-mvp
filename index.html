What we do

Scar & skin recovery

Researching science-backed approaches designed to support skin appearance, comfort, and recovery-related product concepts.

Functional skin care

Exploring consumer-centered formulations and device-enabled concepts focused on usability, quality, and evidence.

Home health diagnostics

Developing at-home testing concepts and digital interpretation pathways for wellness and health-management use cases.

Pet care

Designing practical home-care innovations for companion animals, including odor control and health-monitoring concepts.

The TriSeed science approach

  1. Unmet need. We identify everyday health and wellness struggles.
  2. Evidence review. We aggregate and evaluate the scientific literature.
  3. Concept generation. We design highly usable, practical solutions.
  4. Feasibility check. We analyze regulatory boundaries and manufacturing potential.
  5. Responsible translation. We plan a safe path into the real world.
